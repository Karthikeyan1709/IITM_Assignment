BFSI (Finance / Risk Analytics)
A digital banking platform processes a massive volume of customer transactions every day while maintaining detailed records of user behavior, including spending habits, credit utilization, account activity, and transaction frequency. The institution has observed that customer financial activity varies widely based on demographic factors, credit limits, and historical usage patterns.
Objective: The objective is to estimate and understand customer spending behavior in order to support financial planning, revenue forecasting, and better credit decision strategies.
Challenges: The main challenges include high behavioral diversity across customers, nonlinear relationships between credit usage and spending, and the presence of outliers due to extreme transaction behavior or irregular financial activity.
